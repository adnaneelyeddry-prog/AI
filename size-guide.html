Size Guide

All measurements in inches. Our tees run true-to-size unless noted as Oversized or Boxy fit.

How to Measure

  • A Width — Lay flat, measure armpit to armpit across the chest.
  • B Length — From the highest point of the shoulder to the bottom hem.
  • C Sleeve — From shoulder seam to the end of the sleeve.

Tip: If you're between sizes, size up for a relaxed fit or stay true for a fitted look.

Fabric & Care

  • Material: 100% premium ring-spun cotton, 220 GSM (heavyweight, substantial feel).
  • Pre-shrunk: All tees are pre-washed to minimize shrinkage (less than 3%).
  • Care: Machine wash cold, inside out. Tumble dry low or hang dry. Do not iron directly on prints.
