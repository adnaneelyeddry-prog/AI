Shipping & Returns

We ship fast, keep it simple, and make returns painless. Here's everything you need to know.

Free Standard Shipping

On all orders over $60. Arrives in 4–7 business days to most locations worldwide.

Express Delivery

Need it faster? Express ships in 1–3 business days for a flat rate of $12.

30-Day Returns

Changed your mind? Return unworn items within 30 days for a full refund — no questions asked.

Free Exchanges

Wrong size? Exchange for free. We'll even cover the return shipping label.

Shipping Details

Domestic (United States)

  • Standard (Free over $60): 4–7 business days. Orders under $60 ship for $5.95.
  • Express ($12): 1–3 business days. Order by 2PM EST for same-day dispatch.
  • Next Day ($18): Guaranteed next business day if ordered before 12PM EST.

International

  • Canada & Mexico: 5–10 business days. Free over $80, otherwise $9.95.
  • Europe & UK: 7–14 business days. Free over $100, otherwise $14.95.
  • Rest of World: 10–21 business days. Flat rate $19.95, free over $120.

Note: All international orders may be subject to local customs duties and taxes, which are the responsibility of the recipient. Delivery times are estimates and may vary due to customs processing.

Order Processing

All orders are processed within 24–48 hours of placement (excluding weekends and holidays). Once shipped, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number.

Returns & Exchanges

Return Policy

We want you to love your variant. If something doesn't work out, here's how our return policy works:

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with all tags attached.
  • Returns must be initiated within 30 days of delivery.
  • Custom "Variant Lab" items are final sale (since they're made to your specs) unless there's a defect.
  • Sale items may be exchanged for store credit only.

How to Return

  1. Initiate: Email returns@vrnt.store with your order number and reason.
  2. Ship: We'll send a prepaid return label within 24 hours (free for exchanges, $4.95 deducted for refund returns).
  3. Refund: Once we receive and inspect your item, your refund is processed within 3–5 business days to your original payment method.

Exchanges

Need a different size or color? Exchanges are always free. Follow the same return process and let us know what you'd like instead — we'll ship the new item as soon as your return is scanned by the carrier.

Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged or defective, email us at support@vrnt.store with photos within 7 days of delivery. We'll send a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund — your choice.

FAQ

Can I change my order after placing it?

If your order hasn't shipped yet, we can make changes. Email support@vrnt.store within 2 hours of placing your order.

Do you ship to PO boxes?

Yes! Standard shipping delivers to PO boxes. Express and Next Day require a physical address.

What if my package is lost?

If tracking shows no updates for 7+ business days (domestic) or 21+ days (international), contact us and we'll send a replacement or full refund.
