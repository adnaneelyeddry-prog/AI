Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my tracking number?

You'll receive a shipping confirmation email with your tracking number once your order has been dispatched (usually within 24–48 hours of ordering). Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

My tracking hasn't updated

Tracking updates can take 24–48 hours to appear after initial shipment, especially for international orders. If there's no update after 7 business days, contact us and we'll investigate.

Can I change my shipping address?

If your order hasn't shipped yet, email support@vrnt.store immediately and we'll update it. Once shipped, address changes aren't possible.
