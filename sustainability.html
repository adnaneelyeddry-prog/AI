Sustainability

Streetwear shouldn't cost the planet. Here's how we're building a brand that gives back more than it takes.

Our Commitment

The fashion industry is one of the world's largest polluters. We refuse to be part of the problem. Since 2025, we've restructured our entire supply chain to prioritize sustainability at every step — from raw materials to your doorstep.

We're not perfect yet, and we won't pretend to be. But we're transparent about where we are and where we're going.

Materials

100% GOTS Certified Organic Cotton

Every VARAILLY tee is made from Global Organic Textile Standard (GOTS) certified organic cotton. This means:

  • No synthetic pesticides or fertilizers used in farming
  • 70% less water consumption vs. conventional cotton
  • Fair labor standards throughout the supply chain
  • No GMO seeds — natural farming methods only

Water-Based Inks

All our graphic prints use water-based, PVC-free inks that are non-toxic, biodegradable, and produce vibrant colors that last wash after wash without harmful runoff.

Production

Limited Runs, Zero Overstock

Our limited-variant model isn't just for hype — it's a sustainability strategy. By producing in small batches (typically 50–200 units per variant), we virtually eliminate overstock and unsold inventory. In the traditional fashion model, up to 30% of produced garments are never sold.

Zero-Waste Pattern Cutting

Our production partner uses digital pattern optimization to minimize fabric waste during cutting. Off-cuts are upcycled into cleaning rags, stuffing materials, or donated to local textile arts programs.

Fair Manufacturing

We work with two certified production facilities:

  • Los Angeles, CA: WRAP-certified, living wages, located within 10 miles of our HQ for minimal transport emissions.
  • Porto, Portugal: SA8000-certified facility for our European distribution, reducing trans-Atlantic shipping.

Packaging

100% Plastic-Free

Every order ships in:

  • Compostable mailers made from cornstarch (home-compostable within 180 days)
  • Recycled tissue paper printed with soy-based ink
  • Seed paper brand cards — plant them and grow wildflowers
  • Paper tape — no plastic tape anywhere in our fulfillment chain

Shipping & Carbon

Carbon-Neutral Delivery

We offset 100% of shipping emissions through verified carbon offset projects (reforestation in Brazil + renewable energy in India). Every order page shows the carbon footprint of your shipment.

Regional Fulfillment

We ship from two hubs (LA and Porto) to reduce average shipping distances. Our goal by 2027: add a third hub in Tokyo for Asia-Pacific orders.

The Road Ahead

We're committed to continuous improvement. Here's our roadmap:

2026 Goals

Launch garment take-back program. Introduce recycled cotton blends. Achieve B-Corp certification application.

2027 Goals

100% renewable energy across all operations. Third fulfillment hub in Asia. Full supply chain traceability via QR codes.

2028 Vision

Climate positive operations — removing more carbon than we produce. Circular fashion model with variant-recycling program.

Our Sustainability Timeline

2024 — Awareness

Began auditing our supply chain. Identified key areas for improvement in materials and packaging.

2025 — The Switch

Transitioned to 100% organic cotton, eliminated all plastic packaging, and achieved carbon-neutral shipping.

2026 — Scale & Transparency

Publishing our first annual sustainability report. Launching garment take-back and recycling programs.

Transparency Report

We publish an annual sustainability report detailing our environmental impact, factory audits, and progress against targets. We believe transparency builds trust, and you deserve to know exactly where your tee comes from.

Questions about our sustainability practices? Email sustainability@varailly.store — we're an open book.
