Our Hiring Process

  1. Apply: Send your resume/portfolio via email or the Apply button. No cover letters required — just show us your work.
  2. Quick Chat (15 min): A casual video call to see if the vibes align. No trick questions.
  3. Skills Task (2–4 hrs): A paid take-home challenge related to the role. We pay $150–$300 for your time.
  4. Team Meet: Meet 2–3 team members you'd work with daily. Culture fit goes both ways.
  5. Offer: Usually within 48 hours of the final round. We move fast.

Total timeline: typically 1–2 weeks from application to offer. No ghosting — we reply to every application.
